# Acme Corp — IT Support & Security Policy (v2.1, effective Jan 2026)

## 1. Password Policy

All company accounts require a minimum password length of 14 characters, including at least one
uppercase letter, one number, and one special character. Passwords must be changed every 90 days
and cannot reuse any of the last 8 passwords. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for
all accounts with access to production systems or customer data.

## 2. VPN and Remote Access

Employees connecting to internal systems from outside the office network must use the company VPN
client. VPN sessions automatically time out after 8 hours of inactivity. Split-tunneling is
disabled by default; exceptions require IT Security approval.

## 3. Software Installation Policy

Employees may not install software from outside the approved Software Catalog on company-issued
laptops without IT approval. Requests for new software are reviewed within 3 business days.
Open-source packages used in company codebases must pass an automated license-compliance scan
before merging.

## 4. Incident Response — Severity Levels

- **Severity 1 (Critical):** Full production outage or active security breach. Target response
  time: 15 minutes. Must be escalated to the on-call Incident Commander immediately.
- **Severity 2 (High):** Major feature outage affecting many users, no full outage. Target
  response time: 1 hour.
- **Severity 3 (Medium):** Minor feature degradation or affects a small user segment. Target
  response time: 4 business hours.
- **Severity 4 (Low):** Cosmetic issues, non-urgent requests. Target response time: 2 business
  days.

## 5. Ticket Handling

All IT requests must be logged through the IT Service Desk ticketing system rather than direct
messages to IT staff. Tickets are auto-assigned based on category. Employees will receive an
email confirmation with a ticket ID upon submission. Ticket status can be checked anytime via
the self-service portal.

## 6. Data Backup Policy

All company laptops are backed up automatically to the corporate cloud backup service every 24
hours. Employees storing work files outside the approved cloud storage locations (e.g., personal
USB drives, personal cloud accounts) are in violation of policy and may face disciplinary action.

## 7. Device Return Policy

All company-issued devices (laptops, phones, access badges) must be returned to IT within 5
working days of an employee's last working day. Failure to return equipment may result in a
deduction from the final settlement equal to the device's depreciated value.

## 8. Acceptable Use

Company IT resources are provided for business use. Limited personal use (e.g., checking personal
email during breaks) is permitted as long as it does not interfere with work duties or violate any
other company policy, including the Code of Conduct.
