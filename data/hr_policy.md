# Acme Corp — HR Policy Handbook (v3.2, effective Jan 2026)

## 1. Leave Policy

### 1.1 Annual Leave
Every full-time employee accrues 18 days of annual (paid) leave per calendar year, credited at
1.5 days per completed month of service. Annual leave cannot be carried forward beyond 5 days
into the next calendar year; any unused balance above 5 days is forfeited on December 31st unless
the employee's manager approves a carry-forward exception in writing.

### 1.2 Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required
for any sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days. Unused sick leave does not carry forward and is
not paid out on exit.

### 1.3 Maternity and Paternity Leave
Maternity leave is 26 weeks of fully paid leave for the birth parent. Paternity leave is 2 weeks
of fully paid leave, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth or adoption.

### 1.4 Leave Request Process
All leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 3 working days in advance,
except for sick leave, which may be reported on the day of absence. Leave exceeding 5 consecutive
days requires manager approval at least 7 working days in advance.

## 2. Work-From-Home Policy

Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week without prior approval. Fully remote
arrangements require written approval from both the reporting manager and HR, and are reviewed
every 6 months.

## 3. Expense Reimbursement

### 3.1 Travel Expenses
Domestic travel is reimbursed at actual cost with receipts, up to INR 5,000 per day for lodging
and INR 1,500 per day for meals. International travel requires pre-approval from the department
head before booking.

### 3.2 Reimbursement Submission Deadline
All expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Claims
submitted after 30 days require Finance Director approval and are processed only on an
exception basis.

## 4. Probation Policy

New employees are on probation for the first 3 months of employment. During probation, notice
period for resignation or termination is 2 weeks. After confirmation, the standard notice period
is 60 days for employees at Manager level and above, and 30 days for all other levels.

## 5. Code of Conduct — Confidentiality

Employees must not disclose confidential company information, client data, or trade secrets to
any third party, both during employment and for a period of 2 years after leaving the company.
Violation may result in immediate termination and legal action.

## 6. Performance Review Cycle

Formal performance reviews occur twice a year: a mid-year check-in (June) and an annual review
(December), which determines the annual salary increment and bonus eligibility. Employees rated
"Needs Improvement" in the annual review are placed on a 90-day Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).
